Are you ready to code?

Coding4Cousins is a learning website made to teach the foundations of coding in simple ways.

What We Learn

HTML is the foundation for any website. It makes up the content of your pages.

CSS is the stylistic part of your website. It makes up the design of your pages.

Javascript is what helps your website perform certain actions. It makes up the behavior of your pages.

There's many more things to learn about coding. If you're willing, I want to help you learn it all!
